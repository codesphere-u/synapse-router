Frequently Asked Questions

Architecture & safety guarantees

How does Synapse-Router guarantee output quality on cheaper tiers?

ResponseValidator verifies JSON schema constraints, non-empty tool arguments, absence of refusals, and confidence >= min_confidence. Any failure triggers automatic escalation up the cascade.

What does Nexus Aegis G2 export prove?

export_aegis_g2 produces canonical G2_BUDGET_ROUTING payloads with RFC 6962 0x00-prefixed SHA-256 leaf hashes enforcing USD caps, 120,000-token ceilings, and anti-overprovisioning checks.
